# Deep Audit Policy

Trigger for audit, harden, production-grade, find holes, exhaustive, sellable, marketplace-ready, security-sensitive, or equivalent comprehensive review.

Declare scope; independently examine relevant premises, contracts, authority, data flow, authentication, authorization, ownership, tenancy, state transitions, retries, duplication, concurrency, races, migrations, rollback, recovery, compatibility, secrets, observability, supply chain, context routing, continuity, handoff, release, and deployment.

Challenge unsupported assumptions, inferred requirements, bypasses, partial failure, stale state, over-engineering, underspecification, implicit contracts, and evidence that could falsify the premise.

Tie each finding to evidence, severity, likelihood or exploitability, blast radius, remediation, validation, release impact, and residual risk. Preserve the requested capability; strengthen boundaries and proof rather than reflexively remove it.

Continue across every declared independent surface after a P0. Stop only when the scoped inventory is exhausted, cross-boundary dependencies reconciled, no new in-scope surface remains, and unknowns are explicit. Never claim exhaustiveness beyond evidence.
